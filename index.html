Research

Assessment of the Cellular Actin Cytoskeleton During Disease

University of Manchester · 2024–2025 · Supervisor: Stavros Panagiotou
  • Built an in vitro BBB model: primary HBMECs co-cultured with astrocytes on Transwell inserts.
  • Applied inflammatory/viral/bacterial stimuli ± hypoxia; tracked barrier dysfunction via TEER time series.
  • Quantified F-actin and junction proteins (e.g., ZO-1, occludin) by immunofluorescence + microscopy.
  • Two-way ANOVA: stimuli significantly disrupted BBB; hypoxia consistently exacerbated damage.

Amplification & Extraction of L-asparaginase (AnsB) Protein

Protein Expression & Microbial Techniques · 2023–2024 · Supervisor: Dr. Yi Pan
  • PCR amplification, plasmid construction, and bacterial transformation.
  • E. coli selective culture using aseptic technique; verified expression with SDS-PAGE.
  • Protein extraction/purification via ultrasonication and ion-exchange chromatography.
